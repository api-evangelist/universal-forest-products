GRAND RAPIDS, Mich., Aug. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — UFP Industries, Inc. (Nasdaq: UFPI) today announced plans to establish a new UFP Packaging manufacturing facility in Cherokee County, South Carolina, expanding its Structural Packaging capacity and strengthening its ability to serve national industrial customers with custom, value-added packaging solutions.
